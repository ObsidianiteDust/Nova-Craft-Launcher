*   **正确示范**：`NCL-XX服务器专属版`、`Nova Craft Launcher (某某修复版)`。
*   **错误示范**：`NCL Plus`、`超级启动器`、`XX启动器（完全没提NCL）`。

## 🇬🇧 English

NCL is open-sourced under **GNU GPLv3**. We highly embrace the community and allow free modifications. However, to protect users from deception, we add minimal terms for **anti-confusion** and **attribution**.

### ✅ You CAN (Very Free):
1. **Use and Share**: Download, play, and redistribute the original NCL for free.
2. **Modify**: Change the source code or build your own launcher based on it.
3. **Charge for Distribution (Conditional)**: You may even sell your modified version (allowed by GPLv3), **PROVIDED THAT** you open-source your modified code and do not falsely claim it is official.

### ❌ You CANNOT (Anti-Shell/Scam):
1. **Go Closed-Source**: If you distribute a modified version, you **MUST** open-source it.
2. **Remove Attribution**: Do not remove the original NCL team's copyright notices.
3. **Use Confusing Names**: Do not use `NCL2`, `NCL Official`, `NCL Mobile`, `NCL Pro`, etc.
4. **Impersonate for Fees**: Do not use the "NCL Official" name to charge installation fees or force donations.

### ⚠️ For Forks/Derivatives (Rules for Devs):
If you modify and distribute NCL, your software name or prominent UI area **MUST contain the "NCL" or "Nova Craft Launcher" identifier, plus a suffix indicating it's a third-party modification.**
*   **Correct**: `NCL-Community-Fork`, `Nova Craft Launcher (XX Fix)`.
*   **Incorrect**: `NCL Plus`, `SuperLauncher` (no mention of NCL).
*   **Disclaimer**: As long as you follow these rules, any technical or legal issues are solely your responsibility.

### 🕵️‍♂️ How can normal users identify "Scam/Illegal" versions?
If a software has these traits, it **violates NCL's license**:
1. **No Source Code**: Claims to be based on NCL, but no modified source code is publicly available.
2. **Word Games**: Named "NCL Official" or "NCL 2", but not from the official team.
3. **Malicious Fees**: Selling "NCL Official Installers". **(NCL is 100% free!)**
